# Habit Tracker

## Vision
A playful, celebration-focused habit tracker that makes daily progress feel rewarding. Built for individuals who want to build consistent habits with visual feedback and weekly insights. Gamified motivation meets functional tracking.

## Design
**Direction:** Playful Celebration — vibrant accents, encouraging copy, reward-driven momentum

**Colors (HSL):**
- `--primary: 340 100% 70% (coral pink #FF6B9D)`
- `--accent: 190 100% 50% (cyan #00D9FF)`
- `--achievement: 45 100% 50% (amber #FFB800)`
- `--background: 40 100% 98% (soft cream #FFFBF5)`
- `--foreground: 215 25% 27% (dark slate #2D3748)`
- `--muted: 40 60% 95% (light cream for cards)`
- `--success: 142 71% 45% (green)`

**Typography:**
- Headings: Fredoka (400, 600) — rounded, playful
- Body: Nunito (400, 600) — geometric sans, readable

**Style:** Rounded corners (md-lg), generous spacing, stagger entrance animations, encouraging micro-copy, card-based layout with floating actions.

## Features
- Daily habit check-in interface with streak counters
- Visual progress tracking with celebration states
- Weekly summary reports with insights
- Habit management (add, edit, archive)
